# Photorealism source register

Accessed 2026-09-09. Companion to the [research report](photorealism-research.md).

This is a structured scoping review of public evidence, not an exhaustive systematic review or an independent reproduction study. Paper results are **author-reported**. Product capabilities are **first-party claims** unless stated otherwise. No weights were downloaded, models executed, paid accounts opened, or proprietary systems benchmarked. Public documentation cannot establish what unpublished company systems can do.

Dates below identify the publication/venue where established. A page's crawl date is not its publication date. Code availability does not imply that its dependencies, weights, or generated assets have the same license. Availability was checked at the level stated, not installation-tested.

## Search execution and stopping rule

The methodology was written at the start of the task before the first web search. External research then used these successive query families:

1. Depth-guided scene inpainting/reprojection; multiview PBR texturing; simulator neural enhancement; relightable Gaussians.
2. First-party spatial-world products, current NVIDIA rendering research, and Intel's earlier simulator-image enhancement.
3. SceneTex, RoomTex, RoomPainter, and SceneCraft, including their limitations and code availability.
4. Current object material systems: Hunyuan3D-2.1, TRELLIS.2, Material Anything, MatMart, Paint3D, MVPaint, FlashTex, and DreamMat.
5. Intrinsic decomposition and hybrid rendering: RGB↔X, DiffusionRenderer, MatSpray, TRON, and multiview relighting.
6. Novelty checks using counterfactual/material/relighting queries, following references to Neural LightRig and newer work.
7. Real transfer and strong baselines: SplatSim, RoboGSim, domain randomization, Infinigen, physical rendering, and scanned materials.
8. First-party image-model and interactive-world documentation to distinguish a current product capability from a research claim.

Primary papers, author pages, official repositories, proceedings, and first-party documentation support the report. Secondary results were used for discovery only. The investigation stopped after each major family had relevant primary evidence, the closest proposed-method precedents had been examined, and additional results were mainly variants of covered mechanisms. This is saturation for a direction-setting decision, not proof that the literature is exhausted.

Outstanding uncertainty is concentrated in **comparative output quality on this repository's scenes**, **model/asset compatibility**, **real target-camera transfer**, and **novelty of the narrower intervention-based proposal**. Those require experiments and deeper focused work; more unsorted paper names would not resolve them.
